This policy explains which personal data the mobile app **Tree Smashers** processes, why, and what rights you have. Versions of the game played on web portals are covered by the respective portal's privacy policy.

## 2. Game progress on your device

Your progress (stages, coins, upgrades, settings) is stored locally on your device. We have no access to it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 3. Online leaderboard

When you clear a stage, the app sends the following to our leaderboard server so the global leaderboard can show your rank:

- a random player ID that the app creates on your device (not linked to your name, account or device identifiers),
- your display name (generated automatically, or the name you entered),
- your best stage, team power and team setup.

Your display name and results are visible to other players on the leaderboard. The server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA), which processes your IP address to deliver the request; we do not store it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the leaderboard you use). The entries are kept until you ask us to delete them (see section 8).

## 4. Advertising

The app is free and financed by advertising: videos you choose to watch for an in-game reward, and full-screen ads between battles. Ads are delivered through **Unity LevelPlay** (formerly ironSource; Unity Technologies), which selects an ad from the following networks:

- **ironSource Ads** – Unity Technologies ([privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy))
- **Unity Ads** – Unity Technologies ([privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy))
- **Google AdMob** – Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC ([privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [how Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites))

To show, measure and protect ads these providers process, on their own responsibility:

- device identifiers: on Android the advertising ID and the app set ID; on iPhone and iPad the identifier for vendors (IDFV) — the app never asks for permission to track you, so the providers do not receive your IDFA,
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/region),
- device and app information (model, operating system, language, network type, app version),
- ad interaction data (which ads were shown, watched to the end or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Purposes: delivering and capping ads, measuring their performance, preventing fraud, and – only where you have agreed – personalised advertising.

### Your choice (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

When you first start the app in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether the ad providers may store and access information on your device and show personalised ads. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). If you do not agree, you only receive limited, non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under **Options → Privacy Settings** in the app.

### Everywhere

On Android you can reset or delete your device's advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iPhone and iPad the app does not use App Tracking Transparency and has no access to your advertising identifier. The providers may transfer data to the USA; they rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. Permissions

On Android the app uses the Internet and network-state permissions (for the leaderboard and ads) and the advertising ID permission (for ads, see section 4); on iOS it asks for no permissions. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

## 6. No in-app purchases, no accounts

The app has no in-app purchases and no user accounts, and it does not use analytics tools of its own.

## 7. Children

Tree Smashers is not directed to children; it is intended for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data about them should be deleted, contact us and we will delete the leaderboard entries concerned.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing, and to withdraw a consent at any time with effect for the future. To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us your display name. For data processed by the ad providers, please also use the options described in their privacy policies. You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.

### How to delete your data

- **Leaderboard entry** (player ID, display name, best stage, team power and team setup): email [koststone@gmail.com](mailto:koststone@gmail.com?subject=Tree%20Smashers%20data%20deletion) with the subject “Tree Smashers data deletion” and the display name shown for you on the leaderboard. We delete the entry within 30 days and keep no copy.
- **Game progress on your device**: open Options → Reset game progress in the app, or uninstall the app. We never receive this data.
- **Advertising data**: on Android, reset or delete your advertising ID in the settings (on iOS the app has no access to it), and use the deletion options in the privacy policies of the ad providers listed in section 4.

## 9. Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
