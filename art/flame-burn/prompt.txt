BACKGROUND — this matters more than the style.
Fill every pixel that is not fire, ember or smoke — including the empty middle of every circle — with solid, flat, pure magenta #FF00FF. Treat it as a green-screen: one flat chroma-key colour, edge to edge.
· EXACTLY #FF00FF — red 255, green 0, blue 255. Not pink, not rose, not mauve, not a soft or tinted version of it.
· NOT transparent (a transparency checkerboard gets baked into the artwork). NOT white, cream, black, or any tinted or textured ground.
· No drop shadow onto the background, no vignette.
· KEEP ANY GLOW TIGHT: light belongs inside each flame's own outline. A halo or bloom spreading into the magenta cannot be cut out and ships as a pink smear.
