# Tree Smashers

## Short description (133 / 150 chars)

Flick cute but angry plush animals into battle! Level up 12 smashers, unlock wild skills and smash the enemy's tree to win the fight.

## Description (486 / 500 chars)

Tree Smashers is a turn-based flick-and-smash battler. Pull back your stuffed-animal smashers on their spinning discs and launch them across cozy forest arenas full of islands, speed boosters and sticky mud holes. Knock out every enemy smasher, or crush their Team Tower first for an instant win! Recruit 12 unique heroes, from a spiky hedgehog to a stubborn boar, level them up with coins, unlock three special skills each and fight your way through an endless campaign full of bosses.

## How to play

- Each battle is two teams of smashers taking turns. On your turn, pick one of your smashers and launch it.
- You start with one smasher; a second joins after you win stage 3, a third after stage 10. Choose 1, 2 or 3 in the Team menu: a lone smasher hits harder and has more HP (+20 %), a trio fields smaller smashers that can bump into each other.
- Ram the enemy smashers: faster hits deal more damage. A smasher at 0 HP is out.
- Win by knocking out all enemy smashers **or** by destroying the enemy Team Tower (the tree on their side). Protect your own tree!
- Use the arena: bounce off islands and walls, roll over speed boosters for extra power, and avoid mud (slows you) and deep holes (stop you dead).
- Collect power-ups that appear on the floor for bonus attack, defense or speed during the match.
- Earn coins from battles and spend them on leveling your smashers. Higher levels boost stats; skill 2 unlocks at level 10 and skill 3 at level 20.
- Unlock new smashers as you progress through the campaign. Every 5th stage is a boss fight with special abilities.
- Build your team of one, two or three in the Team menu — every smasher has different strengths and weaknesses.

## Controls

- **Touch / Mouse:** press on one of your smashers, drag backwards to aim (like a slingshot) and release to launch. The further you pull, the stronger the launch.
- **Cancel a shot:** drag back onto your smasher before releasing.
- **Menus:** tap / click buttons; the Team menu lets you swap smashers, level them up and pick colourways.

## Features

- 12 unique plush smashers with their own stats and 3 skills each
- Level-up system with unlockable skills at level 10 and 20
- Team Towers: smash the enemy's tree for an instant victory
- 3 forest arenas with islands, speed boosters and pot holes
- Endless campaign with boss fights every 5 stages
- Leaderboard, league and PvP battles
- Daily rewards, battle pass and colourway skins
- Translated into 39 languages
